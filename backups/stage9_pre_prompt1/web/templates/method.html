Documented Structural Limitations & Sampling Bounds
  • Reduced Sampling Budget: Shapley attribution is evaluated using 50 subset samples and 3 permutations per node to bound runtime to <2 minutes per scenario.
  • Target Averaging Dilution: In scenarios like P01 and P06, averaging Shapley scores across multiple target nodes dilutes signal mass, resulting in a NOISE tier despite Stage 7 detecting single-target spikes.
  • Zero Baseline Variance (INC_07): In synthetic training data, Supplier 5 never exhibited partial receipts (fill_rate = 1.0 constant). Linear SCM fits β = 0.0, rendering upstream attribution mathematically impossible.
  • Sourcing Bug & Two-Way Attribution Reporting: Two of the ten designed scenarios (INC_01 and INC_05) did not produce an effect because of a sourcing bug found in our own audit. The results are reported with and without them: AC@1 is 33.3% across all 10 scenarios versus 50.0% across the 4 valid real-cause scenarios; AC@3 is 66.7% versus 100.0%; MRR is 0.5620 versus 0.7431.
  • Expert Graph Sourcing vs Actual Routing: The expert graph allows supplier links that the simulated sourcing never used (e.g., theoretical links to backup vendors Sri Materials Pvt Ltd and Bharat Steel Works). Stage 9 cause-chain tracing dynamically restricts traversal to active supplier links verified in historical purchase orders.
  • Lag-State Dominance (INC_09): Autoregressive inventory continuity (lag1) captures 60–80% of Shapley variance during prolonged stockouts, suppressing the exogenous supplier delay rank.
  • Cryptographic Immutability: Stages 1–8 are sealed under frozen JSON digests (stage7/FROZEN.json and stage8/FROZEN.json).
