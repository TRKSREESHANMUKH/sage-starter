About this analysis & operational limits

What SAGE does: SAGE performs controlled synthetic causal-attribution evaluation under an expert-defined cause-and-effect map. It continuously cross-checks incoming operational telemetry against physical constraints (Bills of Materials, lead times, workcenter capacities) and calculates statistical consistency scores.

What SAGE does not claim: Real enterprise operations lack ground-truth labels. SAGE does not claim to prove real-world causality. Root-cause candidates reflect structural plausibility relative to human expert models, not mathematical certainty.

Prerequisites for plant deployment: Plant managers must review and validate the factory causal DAG. Operational remediation choices remain the sole responsibility of authorized operations staff.
