How the Demo Starts (Opening Position Setup)

To evaluate SAGE's operational detection on a live multi-day feed, the ten-day simulated sequence (1–10 Jan 2026) begins from an audited opening operating baseline established on 31 Dec 2025. As pre-registered in stage10/demo_seed_manifest.json:

  • 48 Stale Customer Backorders (652 units total): Accumulated unfulfilled 2025 sales orders with requested delivery dates ≤ 2025-12-31 were formally cancelled at year-end closing, preventing historical standing backlog from drowning out new operational anomalies.
  • Purchase Order #18 Cancelled (407 units): A stuck historical PO (139 days overdue, expected 2025-08-14) was cancelled to establish clean supplier open-order tracking.
  • Stock Adjustment #484 (+206 kg Steel): Closing steel balance was 9 kg; seed stock adjustment #484 added +206 kg to establish clean working inventory at 215 kg Steel Rod 10mm (Item 101, safely above 200 kg reorder point). Plastic Granules (Item 102, 204 kg) and Copper Wire (Item 103, 184 meters) naturally carried forward their actual positive year-end balances above safety thresholds without requiring synthetic adjustments.

Day 1 Stock Reconciliation: Early planning drafts cited target inventory levels of 420/310/250 (and 215/140/110 in draft notes); the verified transactional ERP closing inventory on 2025-12-31 and opening Day 1 balance is Steel: 215 kg / Plastic: 204 kg / Copper: 184 meters.

These year-end adjustments are fully audited and accounted for in the Insights reconciliation table under the dedicated "Cancelled at demo opening position" category so that 100% of the 2025 transaction ledger reconciles without hidden drops.

Fulfillment & On-Time Delivery Definition

An order is on time if and only if it is shipped complete on or before its customer-requested delivery date; unfulfilled orders and orders encountering stockout remain in open backlog and are classified as not on time.

Across the 2026 operational sequence (1–10 Jan 2026), 20 customer sales orders were placed for 2026 delivery; all 20 were delivered on their requested delivery date (20/20 on time, 100.0%). The 9 orders placed in late 2025 with delivery dates in early 2026 (109 units) remained unfulfilled in customer backlog, rising to 9 orders (109 units) by Day 7.

How Today's Report is Built
Automated Operational Pipeline

Every daily briefing is synthesized strictly through an automated four-stage pipeline without manual intervention. All operational thresholds, prioritization weights, and causal scoring parameters are versioned and pre-registered in stage10/REPORT_PARAMETERS.md.

1. Snapshot Engine

Evaluates transactional ERP state ≤ as_of (zero lookahead). Computes inventory, backlogs, and receipts.

2. Deterministic Priorities

Applies Rules 1–5 to rank top 5 operational actions by urgency and role assignment (Planner, Procurement, Plant Manager).

3. Causal Trace & Check

Walks upstream DAG in trailing 7-day window. Tags confidence (Seen end to end / Partly seen) and evaluates model agreement.

4. Executive Phrasing

Generates concise briefing text with fact-grounding validator. Priority actions are never drafted by the LLM.

Current Tier Assignment Rules

Evaluated in strict precedence order:

  • CRITICAL: Raw material stock ≤ 0, OR stock ≤ safety stock with overdue PO, OR customer backlog rising with traced cause.
  • NEEDS ATTENTION: Standing overdue backlog steady, OR overdue PO exists, OR daily production shortfall > 0.
  • WATCH: Elevated customer demand intake (≥ 10 units), OR partial goods receipt today (fill rate < 100%).
  • NORMAL: All operational signals within standard parameters.

Weekly Order Forecasting Protocol & Deployed Model
Pre-Registered Protocol v3

The forward-look card on the Today page projects incoming customer demand across a rolling 7-day planning horizon. As pre-registered in stage10/prereg_forecast_v3.md:

  • Deployed Model (Trailing 28-day mean): The deployed production model is a trailing 28-day mean multiplied across the 7-day horizon. It was selected strictly because it achieved the lowest cross-validation WAPE (0.55 / 45.5% accuracy) during walk-forward validation inside the H1 2025 training window.
  • Machine Learning Benchmark: Advanced statistical models, including Negative Binomial count regression and Quantile Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDT), did not beat the trailing 28-day mean on this one-year simulated dataset due to high intermittency (68.2% zero-order days).
  • Theoretical Oracle Limit vs. Deployed Performance: Independent Monte Carlo simulation over 100,000 weeks proves that discrete Bernoulli order arrivals and random order sizing impose an irreducible theoretical aleatoric error floor of 0.50 WAPE (an irreducible 50% accuracy ceiling). The deployed model achieves 0.65 WAPE on out-of-sample weekly tests, operating within 0.14 WAPE of the theoretical noise limit.
  • Protocol v4 Target Comparison (Units Due vs Units Placed): As pre-registered in stage10/prereg_forecast_v4.md, forecasting units due in the rolling 7-day window (ydue = known booked + short-notice) achieves 68.5% accuracy (0.3152 WAPE [TEST]) vs 35.3% accuracy (0.6474 WAPE [TEST]) when predicting net new arrivals (yplaced). Headline accuracy is driven primarily by the ERP order book (68.0% to 76.2% of due volume is already booked on origin day D); the model adds only the marginal difference printed. The new-order part S(D) alone is hard to predict (WAPE 0.98; no oracle is computed for S(D)). In the 10-day demo feed, orders use a compressed 1-day lead time, meaning demo-day forecasts are mostly model-based; the demo feed's order rate is about 3x the 2025 rate, so demo-day forecasts are not an accuracy test. The prediction interval is wide (average width 39.4 units).
  • Wide Prediction Range: Because discrete customer demand arrivals are stochastic, the empirical 80% prediction interval is wide. Forecasts provide macro weekly capacity sizing rather than daily precision.
Documented Structural Limitations & Sampling Bounds
  • Reduced Sampling Budget: Shapley attribution is evaluated using 50 subset samples and 3 permutations per node to bound runtime to <2 minutes per scenario.
  • Target Averaging Dilution: In scenarios like P01 and P06, averaging Shapley scores across multiple target nodes dilutes signal mass, resulting in a NOISE tier despite Stage 7 detecting single-target spikes.
  • Zero Baseline Variance (Scenario 7): In synthetic training data, Supplier 5 never exhibited partial receipts (fill_rate = 1.0 constant). Linear SCM fits β = 0.0, rendering upstream attribution mathematically impossible.
  • Sourcing Bug & Transparent Multi-Cohort Reporting: Two of the ten designed scenarios (Scenarios 1 and 5) did not produce an operational effect because of a sourcing bug found in our own audit (routine PO generation locked to default seed 2025-01-01). The causal benchmark is reported across all cohorts (sample size n is small): All 10 scenarios: Hit@1 = 40.0% (4/10), Hit@3 = 60.0% (6/10), MRR = 0.5622; Excluding sourcing bug (8 scenarios): Hit@1 = 50.0% (4/8), Hit@3 = 75.0% (6/8), MRR = 0.6528; Stage 7 evaluated real-cause cohort (6 scenarios): Hit@1 = 33.3% (2/6), Hit@3 = 66.7% (4/6), MRR = 0.5620; Valid non-bugged real-cause cohort (4 scenarios): Hit@1 = 50.0% (2/4), Hit@3 = 100.0% (4/4), MRR = 0.7431.
  • Simulator Settlement Limitation (Late-But-Delivered is Zero by Construction): The synthetic enterprise simulator fulfills customer orders strictly on their requested delivery date. It contains no retroactive fulfillment sweep. Orders encountering stockouts remain permanently backordered rather than being fulfilled late; thus, the 'Shipped Complete AFTER Requested Date' count is zero by construction.
  • Operational Lead-Time Design in 10-Day Demonstration Feed: In the 2025 historical dataset, customer sales orders exhibit a lead time of 3 to 10 days (median 6.0 days). In the 10-day demonstration feed (January 1–10, 2026), incoming sales orders were scripted with short-cycle 1-day lead times (lead_d = 1) to allow full order-to-delivery cycles, stockout impacts, and customer backlog resolution to be observed within the compressed 10-day demonstration window without altering frozen 2025 database records.
  • Expert Graph Sourcing vs Actual Routing: The expert graph allows supplier links that the simulated sourcing never used (e.g., theoretical links to backup vendors Sri Materials Pvt Ltd and Bharat Steel Works). Stage 9 cause-chain tracing dynamically restricts traversal to active supplier links verified in historical purchase orders.
  • Lag-State Dominance (Scenario 9): Autoregressive inventory continuity (lag1) captures 60–80% of Shapley variance during prolonged stockouts, suppressing the exogenous supplier delay rank.
  • Cryptographic Immutability: Stages 1–8 are sealed under frozen JSON digests (stage7/FROZEN.json and stage8/FROZEN.json).
