How Today's Report is Built
Automated Operational Pipeline

Every daily briefing is synthesized strictly through an automated four-stage pipeline without manual intervention. All operational thresholds, prioritization weights, and causal scoring parameters are versioned and pre-registered in stage10/REPORT_PARAMETERS.md.

1. Snapshot Engine

Evaluates transactional ERP state ≤ as_of (zero lookahead). Computes inventory, backlogs, and receipts.

2. Deterministic Priorities

Applies Rules 1–5 to rank top 5 operational actions by urgency and role assignment (Planner, Procurement, Plant Manager).

3. Causal Trace & Check

Walks upstream DAG in trailing 7-day window. Tags confidence (Seen end to end / Partly seen) and evaluates model agreement.

4. Executive Phrasing

Generates concise briefing text with fact-grounding validator. Priority actions are never drafted by the LLM.

Current Tier Assignment Rules

Evaluated in strict precedence order:

  • CRITICAL: Raw material stock ≤ 0, OR stock ≤ safety stock with overdue PO, OR customer backlog rising with traced cause.
  • NEEDS ATTENTION: Standing overdue backlog steady, OR overdue PO exists, OR daily production shortfall > 0.
  • WATCH: Elevated customer demand intake (≥ 10 units), OR partial goods receipt today (fill rate < 100%).
  • NORMAL: All operational signals within standard parameters.

Documented Structural Limitations & Sampling Bounds
  • Reduced Sampling Budget: Shapley attribution is evaluated using 50 subset samples and 3 permutations per node to bound runtime to <2 minutes per scenario.
  • Target Averaging Dilution: In scenarios like P01 and P06, averaging Shapley scores across multiple target nodes dilutes signal mass, resulting in a NOISE tier despite Stage 7 detecting single-target spikes.
  • Zero Baseline Variance (INC_07): In synthetic training data, Supplier 5 never exhibited partial receipts (fill_rate = 1.0 constant). Linear SCM fits β = 0.0, rendering upstream attribution mathematically impossible.
  • Sourcing Bug & Transparent Multi-Cohort Reporting: Two of the ten designed scenarios (INC_01 and INC_05) did not produce an operational effect because of a sourcing bug found in our own audit (routine PO generation locked to default seed 2025-01-01). The causal benchmark is reported across all cohorts (sample size n is small): All 10 scenarios: Hit@1 = 40.0% (4/10), Hit@3 = 60.0% (6/10), MRR = 0.5622; Excluding sourcing bug (8 scenarios): Hit@1 = 50.0% (4/8), Hit@3 = 75.0% (6/8), MRR = 0.6528; Stage 7 evaluated real-cause cohort (6 scenarios): Hit@1 = 33.3% (2/6), Hit@3 = 66.7% (4/6), MRR = 0.5620; Valid non-bugged real-cause cohort (4 scenarios): Hit@1 = 50.0% (2/4), Hit@3 = 100.0% (4/4), MRR = 0.7431.
  • Simulator Settlement Limitation (Late-But-Delivered is Zero by Construction): The synthetic enterprise simulator fulfills customer orders strictly on their requested delivery date. It contains no retroactive fulfillment sweep. Orders encountering stockouts remain permanently backordered rather than being fulfilled late; thus, the 'Shipped Complete AFTER Requested Date' count is zero by construction.
  • Expert Graph Sourcing vs Actual Routing: The expert graph allows supplier links that the simulated sourcing never used (e.g., theoretical links to backup vendors Sri Materials Pvt Ltd and Bharat Steel Works). Stage 9 cause-chain tracing dynamically restricts traversal to active supplier links verified in historical purchase orders.
  • Lag-State Dominance (INC_09): Autoregressive inventory continuity (lag1) captures 60–80% of Shapley variance during prolonged stockouts, suppressing the exogenous supplier delay rank.
  • Cryptographic Immutability: Stages 1–8 are sealed under frozen JSON digests (stage7/FROZEN.json and stage8/FROZEN.json).
