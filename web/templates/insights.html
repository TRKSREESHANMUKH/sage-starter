Weekly Demand Target Benchmark: Known Booked vs Model Forecast (Protocol v4)

Plain-English Attribution: The headline accuracy of the deployed weekly demand forecast is driven primarily by the ERP order book: between 68.0% and 76.2% of customer demand due in rolling 7-day windows is already booked on origin day D (historical median customer lead time is 6.0 days). The predictive model adds only the marginal difference printed. The new-order part S(D) alone is hard to predict (WAPE 0.9848; no separate oracle is computed for S(D)). In the 10-day demo feed, orders use a 1-day lead time, meaning demo-day forward looks are mostly model-based; the demo feed's order rate is elevated (~7.3 vs ~14.6 units/day, 2.0x units ratio; ~0.63 vs 2.2 orders/day, 3.5x orders ratio), so demo-day forecasts are not an accuracy test. Prediction intervals are wide (average width 39.4 units).
